What a recording adds
Pitch rangeHow much your voice rises and falls, in semitones. A narrow range sounds monotone.
PausesHow often you pause and how long. Short pauses sound confident; long gaps lose people.
VolumeWhether your energy stays even, and whether you trail off at the end of sentences.
PaceEstimated words per minute, counted from syllables in the audio.
